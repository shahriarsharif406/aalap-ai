One app. Every kind of need.

Turn on several modes at once — many children have more than one disability.

Speech

Picture tiles, a sentence strip, type-to-speak with AI suggestions.

Hearing

Live captions with picture hints — and a flash + buzz when the child's name is called.

Simple

Fewer, bigger tiles, gentle praise, and a visual First → Then routine.

Vision

High contrast, large UI, "tap once to hear, tap again to choose", spoken navigation.

Switch

One-button scanning for children who can't use a touch screen.

Built for the families the market skips

  • Free of setup labour. Boards are generated, not hand-built by a therapist.
  • 8 languages, properly. English, বাংলা, हिन्दी, اردو, العربية, Español, Français, 中文 — with right-to-left layouts.
  • Offline-first. Installs like an app; boards, routines and voice work without internet.
  • Private by design. Photos are used once and never stored. Settings stay on the device.
  • Share boards by QR. A parent can hand a board to a teacher in one scan.
